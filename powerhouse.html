Marketing and content

The strategic starting point

Most brands in a market of 100,000 people make the same mistake. They treat it like a smaller version of a big market and apply the same content playbook. Polished product shots. Corporate announcements. Broadcast content designed to reach as many people as possible.

The problem is that in a community this small and this connected, broadcasting feels cold. When I assessed Powerhouse's social media I saw a brand that had not yet understood this. The store was perceived as reliable, corporate, unapproachable. Somewhere you went because you needed a washing machine, not somewhere you followed because you wanted to.

Changing that perception was the first job. Everything else followed from it. I built the strategy around three pillars, always in this order:

01
Humanise the brand
Make the staff real people. Make the store somewhere people wanted to walk into. Connection before conversion.
02
Build trust
Demonstrate expertise. Become the authority on tech on the island. Reputation travels faster than any ad campaign.
03
Drive sales
Once warmth and trust are established, commercial content converts at a completely different rate. You cannot shortcut to this step.
